Our Achievements

Proud moments that represent the dedication and talent of the Seniors'26 class.

Mateo showcased his strategic thinking during a school chess tournament, earning recognition after delivering an impressive checkmate. His calm decision-making and concentration made this one of the most memorable academic competition moments of the year.

Science Fair Medal

Eimy and Solangi represented The Roble Academy at a Science Fair, presenting an innovative project that earned them a medal. Their achievement reflected creativity, teamwork, and scientific excellence.

The boys' soccer team became school champions after an exciting final. Rafa scored two goals, while Mateo secured the victory with a successful penalty kick, giving Seniors'26 an unforgettable championship.

Academic Excellence

Beyond sports and competitions, the Seniors'26 class continues to stand out for its commitment to learning, responsibility, and perseverance, building a strong foundation for future success.

Quantitative Research Project

Our entire class successfully completed a quantitative research project, collecting and analyzing data on different topics through surveys and field research. This experience strengthened our analytical skills, teamwork, and understanding of the research process.

Student Elections Coordination

The Seniors'26 class proudly coordinated the school's student elections. From organizing the voting stations to supervising the election process, our class demonstrated leadership, responsibility, and commitment to ensuring a successful and well-organized event.
